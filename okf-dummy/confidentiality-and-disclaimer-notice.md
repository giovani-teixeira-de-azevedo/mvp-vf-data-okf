This section details the legal disclaimer, scope, and confidentiality terms governing the Request for Information (RFI) issued by Toyota Motor Europe NV/SA (TME).

## Notice Provisions

- **Issuer and Purpose**: The RFI is issued by Toyota Motor Europe NV/SA ("TME") solely to gather information regarding available LAN Management Services solutions, providers, and market capabilities.
- **Non-Binding Status**: The document does not constitute a Request for Proposal (RFP), Request for Quotation (RFQ), Invitation to Bid, or an offer or commitment of any kind. It does not obligate TME to purchase any goods or services, award any contract, or pursue any course of action described within.
- **Response Ownership**: Responses submitted in relation to the RFI become the property of TME and may be used to inform subsequent procurement activities (including a formal RFP) at TME's sole discretion.
- **Modification and Cancellation**: TME reserves the right to modify, suspend, or cancel the RFI at any time without liability to any respondent.
- **Confidentiality and Proprietary Information**: All information provided by respondents will be treated as confidential to the extent permitted by applicable law and organizational policy. Respondents should mark any proprietary information accordingly.
